Who I Am

I've spent 4 years getting insurance systems live across 5 countries — Philippines, Vietnam, Thailand, Taiwan, and Guam. Every go-live was different, every client had their own quirks.

My job is simple: understand what the business actually needs, turn it into something developers can build, make sure the data is clean, and walk the users through it so they don't panic on day one.

I work mostly with T-SQL for data validation, JIRA for tracking, and a lot of patience for stakeholder management. When things go wrong — and they always do — I fix it fast.
